    looking for verdicts
    
  

  

  

    every crop stage two put a name to, newest first. click one to see it whole,
    and to open the recording it was taken during. three things bound what can
    appear here:
    
    
      
  • a verdict is about one frame. it is what the classifier made of
      those exact pixels at the configured margin, before the tracker has had
      its say -- so a crop can appear here without an alert ever being
      published, which needs the same subject on confirm_m of the
      last confirm_n looks. the ones whose vehicle did alert are
      green, including crops saved before the
      alert.
    • 
      
  • only moving vehicles are classified at all. stage two runs on
      what the harvest acts on, so a subject parked at the kerb is judged on its
      approach and its departure and not while it sits there.
    • 
      
  • this starts when the verdict started being recorded. crops
      harvested before that carry no name whatever they were.
    • 
    

  






  

    looking for clips
    
    
    
  

  

  

    clips of the moments something happened, kept in addition to the fixed
    windows make record writes. two things about them are worth
    knowing:
    
    
      
  • a clip starts before its trigger. motion fires once the vehicle
      is already in frame, so the first few seconds are held in a ring and
      written out ahead of it. that approach is the part a recall question
      needs.
    • 
      
  • what a clip is named for is a floor, not a verdict. the name
      records what the pipeline established while it was open, and the pipeline
      only looks when the gate fires. motion means
      nothing further was established, which is not the same as nothing further
      being there.
    • 
    

    the disk budget evicts by that name first and only then by age -- unlike the
    harvest, where the oldest crop is the cheapest to lose. a clip holding a
    recognised subject may be the only one there is.
